Mental Health Screening Questionnaire

This tool looks for patterns in how you've been feeling and functioning and produces screening matches to guide a conversation with a clinician.
This is not a diagnosis and not medical advice.

Safety: If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself or someone else, get help now. In the US call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911.
How this works
  • Answer the short "Start Here" questions first — they decide which sections you'll see.
  • You only answer what's relevant to you. Most people answer far fewer questions than the full set.
  • Within a topic, you'll see the key question first; follow-ups appear only if it applies to you.
